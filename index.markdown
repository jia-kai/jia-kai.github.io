Hello! I work on building practical and reliable AI systems, drawing on
programming languages and symbolic reasoning. My research interests span two
threads: building better computer systems (architecture, high-performance
computing, formal reasoning, and software engineering) and applying
computational methods to model and solve real-world problems (machine learning,
graphics, and algorithmic game theory). I earned my PhD in computer science at
MIT, advised by Professor [Martin Rinard](http://people.csail.mit.edu/rinard/){:target="_blank"}.
