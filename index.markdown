Hello! I work on building reliable AI systems, drawing on programming languages
and symbolic reasoning. My research interests span two threads: building better
computer systems (architecture, high-performance computing, formal reasoning,
and software engineering) and applying computational methods to model and solve
real-world problems, including machine learning, graphics, and algorithmic game
theory.

I currently work at [Ecopia AI](https://www.ecopiatech.com/){:target="_blank"}.
I earned my PhD in computer science at MIT, advised by Professor
[Martin Rinard](http://people.csail.mit.edu/rinard/){:target="_blank"}, and hold
a B.E. in computer science from Tsinghua University. Before my PhD in 2019, I
worked at [Megvii](https://en.megvii.com/){:target="_blank"}, where I led the
development of an in-house deep learning framework later open sourced as
[MegEngine](https://github.com/MegEngine/MegEngine){:target="_blank"}.
